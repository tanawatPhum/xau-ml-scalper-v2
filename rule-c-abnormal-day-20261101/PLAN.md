# Prior abnormal day Rule C research spike — 1 November 2026

FINAL FROZEN PROTOCOL. One primary hypothesis and one fixed stop sensitivity; no grid, retuning or version promotion. This is the user-approved next experiment from the prior research report. All files belong to this new folder. Imported A/B versions and registries remain unchanged.

Source: Caporale and Plastun (2021), https://doi.org/10.1007/s11408-021-00380-w. The earlier checked research report describes gold reversal on the next day, but next-day gold trading results were not distinguishable from random and transaction costs were excluded. This experiment is our adaptation, not a reproduction of a proven profitable published rule. In particular the 60-day reference, z threshold, quality limits, clocks and implementation below are ours.

Use only observed, calendar-available BID/ASK mid quotes. The fixed day is UTC+03:00, with no DST. R=log(last observed available mid/first observed available mid) for a completed calendar date. A valid day has at least1000 quotes, first local minute<=120, last local minute>=1320; these three quality rules are declared before outcomes. Holiday-blocked but calendar-available quotes may form daily features; no synthetic or forward-filled prices. A previous date becomes complete only when a later date is actually observed. The last observed date is not finalized at EOF.

At a new date, require the immediately preceding calendar date to be valid: never carry Friday into Monday or a missing date. Sunday short data fail quality. Compare its R with the latest60 valid daily Rs strictly earlier than that previous date; sample SD(ddof1), z=(R-mean)/SD. Require finite positive SD, |z|>2 and nonzero R. Signal opposite the sign of R, not the sign of z. At most one signal on the first actually observed calendar-eligible quote strictly before06:00 that date. If its known positive eff volatility scale is invalid, skip that date; otherwise scale=max(eff*mid/10000,.50 USD/oz). No current-date end/coverage/quote-count condition may gate its signal.

Entry must fill at exactly signal timestamp+1minute on an observed eligible quote, before expiry and daily deadline; otherwise cancel, with no retry. Primary stop and target are disabled. Fixed3scale observed-close stop is a sensitivity only, never a replacement for a failing primary. When a stop is detected, exit at the next actually observed quote. No take profit. A/B entry activity never vetoes C.

Absolute local expiry is06:00 of the signal date. Close at the first actual quote at/after min(that expiry, existing16:55 New York daily close deadline); an observed newly blocked quote is an emergency exit. There is no retrospective gap liquidation, no future-gap lookup, no EOF liquidation, and no fixed hold-from-entry substitute. Unchanged A/B calendar closes16:58NY, reopens18:01(Sunday18:05), and retains its DST/conservative holiday rules. In winter UTC+3midnight=16:00NY: the existing16:55NY flatten truncates the trade near00:55 local, before the break. Report this limitation rather than imply holding through the break. Missing cutoff quotes exit at the first real quote and count as late; any market close/session crossing fails.

Training2010–2016 only, warmup starts2010(no earlier data). One primary passes only if >=50 closed trades, fixed-spread net>0, PF>1,2014–2016 net>0, example-cost net>0, C-entry overlap with A/B holding union<=30%, no market-close/session crossings, no terminal position, positive minimum equity for C and A/B+C at0.1lot. Report0.5lot equity survival separately; it is not an extra selection criterion. Rare counts fail without loosening thresholds. Late cutoff and data-gap counts are reported, not additional selection criteria. All failures are reported.
